# Video Mix DJ Studio

`video-mix-dj-studio` is an account-synced Marketplace package with a responsive dashboard page at `/dashboard/video-mix-dj-studio`, a desktop rail link, and a native iOS route. The iOS route hosts the studio in an inline-media WKWebView, hides the app navigation and tab chrome, and requests the page’s full-screen presentation mode. The mobile-first deck is a fixed, non-scrolling surface: both turntables and their waveforms remain visible in portrait and landscape, while loading, search, mixer, sampler and set actions open compact overlays above it. Loaded video artwork becomes the spinning record label.

The studio accepts a YouTube URL or video id for each deck, detects the common watch, share, Shorts, live, embed, attribution and playlist forms, removes their extra parameters, embeds the canonical video through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced host, and uses the player message protocol for transport, position, tempo and volume. Its in-studio search gets results from a healthy public CORS-enabled Invidious index; selecting a result loads its canonical YouTube video directly into the chosen deck. The equal-power crossfader sets each player’s effective volume and its matching opacity, so sound and picture make the same transition. Each deck’s touch platter pauses, seeks as it is rotated and resumes when released. Capture stores an eight-second source region as a local sampler section; its button seeks and plays the region back from its source deck. The local Web Audio sampler also has eight touch-safe synthesized percussion pads (mapped to `E`, `R`, `T`, `Y`, `U`, `I`, `O` and `P`), so it does not download, upload or retain audio recordings. Cues, captured regions, deck level, tempo, sampler level and the crossfader are retained only in `localStorage`; no package API or worker is present.

The page is intentionally controlled by the selected video’s availability policy. YouTube advertising, embedding rules and any playback restrictions remain under YouTube and the video owner’s control.
