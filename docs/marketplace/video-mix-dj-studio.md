# Video Mix DJ Studio

`video-mix-dj-studio` is an account-synced Marketplace package with a responsive dashboard page at `/dashboard/video-mix-dj-studio`, a native desktop studio, and a native iOS route. The dashboard page's mobile-first deck is a fixed, non-scrolling surface: both turntables and their waveforms stay visible in portrait and landscape, while loading, search, mixer, sampler and set actions open compact overlays above it. Loaded video artwork becomes the spinning record label. Performance checks for iOS are in [video-mix-dj-studio-performance.md](video-mix-dj-studio-performance.md). Part of [Marketplace](README.md).

## iOS

The iOS route is a SwiftUI studio (`native/ios/ForkMesh/Features/Packages/VideoMixDjStudio*.swift`; pure logic and tests in `ForkMeshCore/Sources/FMPackages/DjStudio.swift`), presented as a full-screen cover that hides the shell's bars, prompt, spider, overlays and home indicator. The iOS status bar stays visible so the battery is in view (iPhones hide it in landscape). The back button (top-left; also in the info sheet) closes it in one tap and pops the route. There is no gear menu: loading is on the library handles, deck titles and the platter's load button; deck settings on each pitch strip; pads, Reset All and info in the bottom row; Leave on the back button.

The studio model is app-wide (`DjStudioHub`), so leaving does not stop the music: the shell's top bar shows a spinning record (`DjNowPlayingChip`, `VideoMixDjStudioNowPlaying.swift`) with the loudest playing deck's artwork, cropped to a full circle, filling the bar's 44-point height with no label, grooves, rim or visualizer (`DjSpectrum` and `DjSpectrumMeter` in `FMPackages/DjSpectrum.swift` stay in the library with their tests but nothing draws them). Tapping it reopens the studio; long-press offers Stop the Music. Leaving with nothing playing hands the audio session back.

- **Landscape** is the classic two-turntable layout: a top bar with the back button, each deck's elapsed time and full length (`01:23 / 04:56`; tapping the clock shows the time left), title and channel, and both waveforms; pitch strips on the outer edges (settings, BPM, SYNC, pitch fader, − / + bend) with each deck's volume fader inside them; the two platters with the **queue** between them; a bottom bar with each deck's START / PAUSE / STOP / RESTART and SET / cue / SAMPLE around the crossfader; and at the very bottom the info, pads, playlists, AUTOMIX, AUTO DJ, reactions and Reset All buttons and a tiny diagnostics button (`DjUtilityRow`). Nothing else sits in the middle; looping is in each deck's settings sheet.
- **Portrait** stacks Deck A above Deck B with the queue strip (and, in a jam, the people row) and the crossfader between them, and the same bottom row; each deck has its transport and SET / cue / SAMPLE down its left side and its volume fader between platter and pitch strip.
- **Deck volume** (`DjLevelFader`): a vertical fader per deck with a speaker icon and the level in percent, the same level as the deck settings sheet; double-tap returns it to 100 %; it multiplies with the crossfader. Levels stay on each phone, also in a jam.
- **Transport**: START plays (or opens the loader on an empty deck), PAUSE holds, STOP stops and returns to the beginning, RESTART starts over. PAUSE lights while paused mid-song, STOP while stopped at the start.
- **Repeat** (`DjRepeatButton`, `toggleRepeat`): a round repeat-one button next to each deck's clock, the same switch as "Repeat song" in the deck sheet. While lit, the deck loops its song itself instead of waiting for YouTube's end report (which stops the player, so a seek after it was not heard and the song was skipped as failed): `DjRepeat.span` (`FMPackages/DjParty.swift`) takes the first and last waveform bar (`DjDeck.waveform`, 20 bars a second) louder than `DjRepeat.quiet` (0.07, about 29 dB under the loudest bar) as where the sound starts and ends, and the loop jumps from the trailing silence to the end of the leading silence. Until the waveform is read, or when it is silent or has under 2 s of sound, the span is the whole song up to 0.25 s before YouTube's length. Each tick (`aimRepeat`) asks `DjRepeat.step`: at or past the end it seeks to the start (`rewind(deck, to:)`); within 0.6 s of the end (at the deck's tempo) it sets a timer for the exact moment so the 250 ms ticks leave no gap. The 8-second loop wins while on. A believed end report is still a fallback (sparse background ticks) and also seeks to the sound's start; the host page counts each `seekTo` (`w.seek`) and the frame moves an ended voice to the new position. An early or stale end report never restarts the song; a song that fails to play is not retried. AUTOMIX never mixes out of a repeating deck (`DjAutomixDeck.repeats`); the other deck can still be started, and shake to mix and Next still mix away. Repeat belongs to the deck: it survives loads and ejects, is saved (`DjSavedDeck.repeats`), is cleared by Reset All, stays per phone in a jam (the restart itself is shared like RESTART).
- **SAMPLE** has three steps (`DjSampleStep`): REC (red) from the deck's position, ARMED (blue) on the second tap, then play. A sample is 1 to 30 s (under 0.25 s is discarded) and is also saved to the next of the four sampler pads. Playing it jumps the deck to the sample; a playing deck carries on from its end, a stopped deck stops again.
- While the studio is on screen the app's interface sounds are silenced (`FmCuePlayer.quieten`, Genie chimes included); haptics remain.
- The iPhone app is otherwise portrait-only: `FmOrientationLock` (answered by `AppDelegate`'s `supportedInterfaceOrientationsFor`) allows landscape only while the studio is on screen; iPad keeps every orientation.

Both decks play through YouTube IFrame players in one WKWebView (`DjPlayerHost`) hosted on the server's origin (or `https://forkmesh.com` when signed out), so YouTube's embedding rules apply. The web view takes no touches and is parked behind the app's root view in the key window, so it keeps playing after the studio closes.

YouTube's API only offers 0.25 speed steps and iOS ignores a web player's volume, so a user script (`frameScript`, injected with `forMainFrameOnly: false`) works on the frame's media directly, driven by `postMessage` from the host page:

- it sets `playbackRate` to the deck's tempo (`preservesPitch` off, like vinyl) and restores it whenever YouTube resets it, so pitch, bend and SYNC change the music;
- below full crossfader or deck level it routes the deck through a Web Audio gain node, so the fader really fades; only for routable streams (a cross-origin stream would go silent), otherwise the deck mutes below 2 %;
- while a record is scratched it plays forward at the hand's speed and seeks back for backward strokes (until the deck's audio is read, see scratching below).

**Position** comes from a quarter-second player tick, interpolated at the deck's real rate. The playhead advances only once YouTube reports playing; small drift eases out and jumps over 0.3 s snap (`DjStudio.trackedPosition`). Static views redraw only when inputs change; turntable, waveform and clock timelines pause while a deck is stopped.

The record and waveform are drawn from a display position (`DjDeck.shown`, `DjSpin` in `DjStudio.swift`), so START, PAUSE, STOP and seeks never jerk:

- like a motor, speed ramps toward the deck's rate (0.09 s up, 0.18 s down) and the shown position eases toward the real one (0.2 s);
- the record has its own motor (`DjSpin.turn` and `motor`, read by `DjDeck.turned`): it spins up as soon as START is pressed while the waveform playhead waits for the audio (YouTube can take up to three seconds). Only a held or scratched record follows the position; seeks, cues and song changes do not jump it; a frame stalled under half a second (`DjSpin.hitch`) turns it on by the time missed;
- after PAUSE or STOP the record coasts for 1.5 s (`coasting`) and settles where the music stopped;
- a jump over 0.6 s (seek, cue, restart, new song) moves straight there; a held record follows the hand exactly; the clock reads the real position;
- a frame more than 0.1 s after the previous one (`DjSpin.gap`) jumps straight to the extrapolated position. The waveform draws on every display frame while the deck moves (no 1/60 s minimum interval, which skipped frames on a 60 Hz screen);
- START sends the play command before the deck's bookkeeping (play count, history, jam share, save).

**Touch and scratch.**

- Touching a record only slows it, like a fingertip on vinyl: tempo drops 8 % while the finger rests and for at least 0.35 s after a tap (`touchBegan` / `touchEnded`, `DjStudio.touchBrake`). A scratch starts only after 14 points of movement (or 5 % of the platter, whichever is larger; `DjStudio.isScratch`), so a tap never jumps the track.
- Dragging scratches the song like vinyl, 1.8 s of track per revolution. The audio decoded for the waveform (22.05 kHz mono) is kept as 16-bit samples (`DjScratchAudio`, songs up to 15 minutes, about 2.6 MB a minute; read again when a cached song is loaded). While the record is held the web voice pauses and one `DjScratchVoice` per deck on the sampler's `AVAudioEngine` plays those samples from the hand's position: a critically damped follower (`DjScratchHead`, 22 Hz) moves the read head, so backward strokes play in reverse at the hand's speed and pitch, scaled by crossfader and deck level. Holding still is silent; release seeks the web voice to the hand's position and resumes if the deck was playing. The muted video follows the hand four times a second.
- Until the audio is read (or when it cannot be: WebM, HLS, Low Data Mode, songs over 15 minutes) the hand's speed (`DjStudio.scratchSpeed`) drives the web voice forward and a synthesized voice (band-passed noise following the speed, `DjScratchNoise`) plays, louder on backward strokes. The sampler level sets only the drum pads' volume. The red light below the platter returns the deck to its cue and stops it.

**Waveforms.** Each strip is a 16-second window centred on the playhead; dragging scrubs. They are drawn only from the real audio: when the frame script creates a deck's voice it reports the stream URL (`source`, plain streams only, never HLS); `DjWaveSource` downloads it once (not on Low Data Mode, at most 200 MB) in 2 MB `Range` requests, four at a time (`DjWaveRanges`: YouTube can throttle one whole-stream request to playback speed), decodes it with `AVAssetReaderAudioMixOutput` at 22.05 kHz mono, and `DjWaveAccumulator` makes 20 bars a second (1,200 to 12,000; `DjWaveAccumulator.bars`): height is RMS loudness normalised to the loudest bar, colour the balance of bass (below 250 Hz, red), mids (green) and treble (above 2.5 kHz, blue). Bars are cached per video in `Caches/dj-waveforms`; the download is a `dj-wave-<uuid>` temp file deleted once decoded (its single-file removes are on the destructive-actions contract test's allow-list). While reading the strip says "Reading the audio…"; an unreadable stream shows only the centre line and playhead.

**Tempo and BPM.**

- The pitch fader covers ±16 % (up is faster); − / + bend 4 % while held; the BPM box is tap tempo, and SYNC matches this deck's BPM (or tempo, without BPMs) to the other deck. Without a BPM the box shows the tempo change in percent. RESET under each pitch fader centres it (as do a double-tap and Reset in the deck sheet); its label dims while centred.
- **Automatic BPM** (`DjBpm`, `FMPackages/DjBpm.swift`): once the audio is read, the BPM is detected off the main thread from up to two minutes from the middle of the song: an onset envelope (rises in loudness, full band plus a 180 Hz low band for the kick) every 256 samples, its autocorrelation, and a score for every tempo from 70 to 180 BPM in 0.1 steps averaging the autocorrelation at 1 to 8 beats (so 1.5 or 0.5 beat periods score low). A mild preference for tempos near 120 picks between a tempo and its half; the double is taken when it scores at least 80 % as well. The peak is refined to a fraction of a frame and rounded to 0.1. No steady beat (best score under 0.04) gives no BPM. A tapped or saved BPM is never replaced; long-pressing the BPM box (or Detect in the deck sheet) detects again; Clear BPM returns to tap tempo.
- The crossfader is equal power through the Web Audio gain; its arrows slam to one side.

**AUTOMIX** is an on / off mode. A 0.5 s loop asks `DjAutomix.step` (`FMPackages/DjParty.swift`):

- with nothing playing it starts a cued deck (and swings the crossfader to it) or loads the next queued video onto the deck the crossfader favours;
- while a deck plays it preloads the next queued video onto the idle deck;
- with ten seconds left (at the real tempo; `DjAutomix.lead` is 2 s) it starts the other deck, waits until it is really heard (advancing, at most `DjAutomix.audible`, 3 s), crossfades over the outgoing song's time left (2 to 8 s) and pauses the old deck. The crossfader moves every display frame; the incoming song's waveform and scratch audio download only after the fade, so it does not compete with the starting stream; a deck's fader counts only one Web Audio wake-up at a time, so a fade never falls back to the 2 % mute and cuts the song in and out.
- Loading from the queue takes the video off it. A deck counts as cued when loaded, stopped and more than ten seconds from its end, so a just-finished song never replays. A song counts as finished only within five seconds of its end (`DjEnd`): earlier "ended" reports are ignored (one arriving a second into a new song made AUTOMIX load three AUTO DJ picks in four seconds), and if they go on for three seconds the song is skipped like a failed one. A deck's audio read is cancelled when its song leaves the deck.
- PAUSE or STOP that leaves nothing playing (or a jam partner pausing everything), Stop the Music and Reset All turn AUTOMIX off. Holding AUTOMIX offers **Mix to the Other Deck Now** (an eight-second fade).
- **Never runs dry**: with the queue empty it loads from everything played before and everything saved in Playlists (`DjAutomix.next`): not the songs on either deck, shuffled among the older half by last play, falling back to anything not playing. The pick is preloaded like a queued video. A song that failed to play (removed, private, not embeddable) is skipped for the session, and a deck whose song failed counts as empty. AUTOMIX can be kept to one playlist, tag or smart playlist (see Playlists and tags).
- **The button** (`DjAutomixButton`) is a capsule the height of its round neighbours (28 points at the base scale), with a shuffle icon (two arrows during a crossfade); off it is the dark face with a thin blue outline, on it fills with accent blue and glows. Tap area at least 44 × 44 points.

**Shake to mix**: shaking the phone while the studio is open (no sheet up) makes the same crossfade into the other deck if it is playing or cued, otherwise after loading the next queued (or library) song onto it. It ticks and names the song; with nothing playing it says to start a deck; it does nothing during a fade or scratch. The shake is UIKit's gesture: a `UIWindow` extension (`VideoMixDjStudioScreen.swift`) posts `fmDeviceShaken` and passes the motion on (shake to undo still works); the studio listens only while on screen. In a jam AUTOMIX runs on the phone that turned it on; its loads, starts and pauses are shared like any other deck change.

**AUTO DJ** (`DjAutoDjButton`; model glue `VideoMixDjStudioAutoDj.swift`; logic and tests `FMPackages/DjAutoDj.swift`, `DjAutoDjTests.swift`): a magic-wand button after AUTOMIX. While on, the next song is found on the internet: a related song people liked, never the same song. It rides on AUTOMIX (turning AUTO DJ on turns AUTOMIX on, and off again if it was AUTO DJ that turned it on; anything that turns AUTOMIX off turns AUTO DJ off). Each time a new song becomes the live deck's (or, with nothing playing, the latest in Recently Played), `DjStudioModel.primeAutoDj` looks it up once:

- candidates are YouTube's recommendations from the same Invidious instances as search (`/api/v1/videos/<id>?fields=recommendedVideos`), or, when none are left, a search for the artist and genre or title (`DjSmart.searchQuery`);
- `DjAutoDj.eligible` drops the song itself, songs on either deck, Recently Played, failed songs, this session's picks (`autoDjPlayed`, 200) and other uploads of a song already heard (compared by `DjAutoDj.songKey`: bracketed parts, the artist before ` - ` and words such as official, video, lyrics or remastered dropped), and videos under a minute or over 12 minutes;
- only songs qualify: `DjAutoDj.isSong` drops titles or channels naming a talent show or its parts (got talent, AGT, BGT, X Factor, The Voice, American Idol, golden buzzer, auditions, judges, "sings", "performs"), reaction or vocal-coach videos, covers, karaoke or backing tracks, tutorials, remixes, sped-up, slowed, nightcore or 8D edits, mashups, parodies, medleys, interviews, trailers, episodes, compilations, documentaries and live takes (live at / from, concert, Tiny Desk). Live, cover, acoustic, unplugged, instrumental, remix, session or demo count only in brackets, after ` | ` or after a second ` - ` ("Oasis - Live Forever (Remastered)" plays). Dropping a real song costs little; playing a talent show does not;
- originals come first (`DjAutoDj.isOriginal`: the artist's own channel, a YouTube Music `- Topic` track, a VEVO channel, or a title marked official, remastered or original), in `eligible` and in `ranked`;
- the six most related have their likes read (`/api/v1/videos/<id>?fields=likeCount,viewCount` in parallel, `DjAutoDj.likesURL`) and `DjAutoDj.ranked` orders them by log10 of likes (1 % of views when unknown), plus up to 1 for a like-to-view ratio up to 10 %, plus up to 2 for YouTube's own order; the rest follow, ranked the same way.

The best pick is loaded onto the idle deck ("AUTO DJ: … is up next."). Queued songs still play first. AUTOMIX waits for the lookup; a pick that fails gives way to the next; a failed or empty lookup falls back to the library and says so. Turning AUTO DJ on while a deck plays replaces an idle deck's cued song with the pick. Next and shake to mix take AUTO DJ's pick too, and a shake never mixes back into a song the other deck already played (`DjDeck.counted`) while something else can be loaded. AUTO DJ is saved with the studio (`DjSaved.autoDj`, only with AUTOMIX); the diagnostics read-out shows its state.

**Library and state.**

- The sampler sheet replays the four samples and has eight synthesized drum pads, rendered once into `AVAudioEngine` buffers and triggered on touch-down.
- Search uses the same public Invidious index as the web page, trying up to three healthy instances. Type and press return: the top result loads onto the deck and the sheet closes; a pasted YouTube link loads directly. Reopening the loader shows the other results (the loaded one ticked).
- **Recently Played** (the latest 40, no duplicates, titles filled in once YouTube reports them; `DjStudio.remember`) is the loader's first list with the search box empty; swipe or Clear removes.
- **Play counts**: every played song is kept in a library (`DjPlayedSong`: id, title, author, plays, last played; latest 500, `DjStudio.played`). A play counts when a deck starts a song it has not counted since it was loaded, stopped to the start, restarted or played to the end, so pause/resume, relaunch, web-view reload and jam corrections never count twice. Rows show "12 plays"; **Most Played** (more than one play, 25 at most) follows Recently Played. Removing a song from either list also removes it from the library.
- Deck videos, titles, cues, tempo, BPM, levels, samples, sampler level, crossfader, queue, history and library are kept in `UserDefaults` (`fm.videoMixDjStudio.v1`) on the device only, encoded and written on a serial background queue (`DjStore`) so the five-second save while playing never holds a frame; backgrounding or closing waits for the queue. The screen stays awake while the studio is open.
- **Resuming**: the saved state holds each deck's position, whether it was playing and whether AUTOMIX was on. A deck's playing intent (`keepsPlaying`) is set by START (and RESTART, AUTOMIX, a jam start) and cleared by PAUSE, STOP, the cue light, Stop the Music, Reset All, a new load, a playback error or the song ending, so a pause iOS or YouTube makes on its own does not count. The state is written on every start and pause, every five seconds while playing, and on backgrounding or close.
  - On relaunch the shell (`HomeShell`, `DjStudioHub.relaunch`) checks once; if a deck was playing and the package is installed it creates the studio in the background, loads each deck at its position and starts the ones that were playing (with AUTOMIX and the remembered jam). A loaded, paused deck comes back paused at its position.
  - Returning to the foreground, or the end of an audio interruption with iOS's resume hint, restarts every deck that should be playing.
  - If the web view's content process is killed (memory pressure in the background), `DjPlayerHost.onReload` reloads each deck at its current position and plays the ones that should be.

### Playlists and tags

`FMPackages/DjCrate.swift` (tests `DjCrateTests.swift`; views `VideoMixDjStudioCrate.swift`, glue `VideoMixDjStudioPlaylists.swift`). The list button in the bottom row opens **Playlists**:

- **AUTOMIX** at the top chooses what AUTOMIX plays once the queue is empty (`DjCrate.focus`): **Everything**, one **playlist**, one **tag** or a smart playlist. With **Shuffle** it picks as above; without, it plays in order from the song after the last pick (`cursor`) and starts over. Choosing a focus while AUTOMIX is on reloads an idle deck whose song is outside it. Holding AUTOMIX also offers the choice (**Plays …**); its icon becomes a target while a focus is set.
- **Playlists**: create, rename, delete; Edit to reorder or remove; **Add Songs** (tick from everything played or saved, or paste a link into the filter); **Play with AUTOMIX**; **Add All to the Queue** and **Shuffle into the Queue** (in a jam, the jam's queue).
- **Tags**: lowercased words without `#`, up to 20 per song, 32 characters each. A tag page has the same actions; renaming onto an existing tag merges. **All Songs** lists every played or saved song with a filter (title, channel or `#tag`); removing a song there removes it from every playlist, its tags and its play count.
- Every song row is a menu: Load on Deck A / B, Add to the Queue, Add to Playlist and **Tags and Playlists…** (toggle tags, add new ones comma-separated, toggle playlists). The same page opens from holding a song in the loader, a queue thumbnail and the deck sheet.
- **Filing from the turntable** (`DjFileButton`, the platter's bottom-right corner): lights blue when the song is in a playlist or tagged; lists every playlist and tag (ticked where the song is), New Playlist…, New Tag…, Tags and Playlists… and, when the song is in the AUTOMIX focus, Take Out. While AUTOMIX is focused on a list holding the song, choosing another list *moves* it (`DjStudioModel.move`); otherwise it is only added. A song that left the focused list while AUTOMIX is on is skipped (`skipOut`): a playing deck crossfades over two seconds into the other deck (loaded first if needed) and the old deck then gets the following song (or is emptied); with both decks playing the deck pauses and the crossfader swings; an idle deck holding it is reloaded. During a crossfade or scratch it is filed but not skipped.
- **Next** (`DjNextButton`, `DjStudioModel.quickNext`, the platter's bottom edge): loads the song AUTOMIX would pick (first queued video, else the next from the focus), never one on a deck or one that failed. A playing deck starts it at once; a paused or empty deck is cued. During an AUTOMIX crossfade it stops the fade, swings back to the other deck if playing and cues the new song. The deck sheet has Load the next song.
- **Find similar** (`DjSimilarButton`, `DjSimilarSheet` in `VideoMixDjStudioSimilar.swift`): suggestions to load on the next deck (the picker starts on the other deck, or Queue when it is playing). **From Your Library** ranks songs by `DjSmart.similar`: same artist 5, each shared tag 3, each shared playlist 2, same genre 2, style or decade 1, with reasons shown. **Suggested on YouTube** uses the video's recommendations (`DjStudio.similarResults`), falling back to an artist/genre or title search. Fetched once per song.
- **Don't Shuffle** (`DjTrack.noShuffle`): such a song is never picked at random (AUTOMIX on Everything, shuffled lists, Next, shake to mix; `DjCrate.pool`), but plays in an ordered playlist, the queue or by hand. Rows show "Not shuffled"; exported as `noShuffle: true` when set.
- **Delete from Library** (every song menu, the loader's hold menu, the file menu and the deck sheet) removes the song from the library, every playlist, its tags, its play count and Recently Played. On a deck it also leaves the local queue and is replaced at once (`skipOut` with AUTOMIX on, otherwise the next song, or the deck is emptied).
- **Smart playlists** (`FMPackages/DjSmart.swift`, tests `DjSmartTests.swift`; views `DjSmartKindView`, `DjSmartView`), recomputed on a background task whenever the library or collection changes (`DjStudioModel.refreshSmart`, kept in `DjCrate.smart`, never saved; newest result kept, immediate when the studio is created). **Listening**: Most Played (more than one play, 50 at most), Recently Played (50), Never Played, Played Once, Rediscover (last played over 30 days ago). **Genres** (house, techno, trance, drum & bass, dubstep, EDM, UK garage, hip-hop, R&B, pop, K-pop, rock, chill / lo-fi, Latin, Afrobeats, reggae, disco / funk, jazz, classical, country) and **Styles** (remixes and edits, DJ sets, live, acoustic and covers, sped up / slowed, official videos, lyric videos) match whole words of title and tags. **Decades** come from a year (1950 to 2029) or `80s`-style words; **Artists** group by channel (without ` - Topic`, `VEVO`, ` Official`, ` Music`) or the title before ` - `, for artists with two songs or more (30 at most). A list needs two songs. A smart page has Play with AUTOMIX, the queue actions, Save as Playlist and Export; the focus is saved as `smart:<id>` (`DjFocus.smart`) and falls back to Everything when the list empties. Filing while AUTOMIX plays a smart list only adds.
- **Export**: **Export Everything** (and Export on a playlist or tag) shares JSON (`format: forkmesh-dj-playlists`, `version: 1`: songs with title, channel and tags; playlists by name with video ids). **Export as M3U** shares YouTube links.
- **Import**: **Import from Files** reads that JSON, M3U, text with YouTube links (one per line) or a JSON list of links or objects with `videoId` / `url` and `title`; **Paste** reads the clipboard. Imports merge: an existing song gains the imported tags, a same-named playlist (case-insensitive) gets new songs appended, and untagged links become a playlist named after the file (or Pasted Songs).

Limits: 2,000 saved songs, 100 playlists, 1,000 songs a playlist, files up to 4 MB. Tagging or adding to a playlist saves the song (id, title, channel). The collection has its own `UserDefaults` key (`fm.videoMixDjStudio.crate.v1`), written on the save queue only when it changes. Reset All and Clear leave it alone; playlists are not shared in a jam, only songs queued from them.

### Queue and jam sessions

The middle of the studio is a queue of thumbnails (`DjQueueView`, `VideoMixDjStudioQueue.swift`). + opens the loader with **Queue** chosen (the picker is Deck A / Deck B / Queue). Tapping a thumbnail, or holding it 0.35 s (haptic tick), offers Load on Deck A / B, Tags and Playlists… or Remove from Queue; loading takes it off the queue. Outside a jam the queue stays on the device.

**Adding with nothing playing**: when no deck plays (and no crossfade or scratch runs), an added song loads and starts at once (`DjStudioModel.playNow`, `VideoMixDjStudioJam.swift`) on the deck the crossfader favours, or, with the crossfader within half way of the middle and that deck holding a song while the other is empty, on the empty deck (`DjAutomix.playNow`, tests in `DjPartyTests.swift`). Adding a playlist or tag plays its first song this way and queues the rest. In a jam the start is shared.

Sharing YouTube links to ForkMesh from the iOS share sheet queues them: when everything shared is YouTube video links and there are no images, the share extension opens the studio without its sheet and `SurfacesCoordinator.pickUpShare` queues each video (`DjStudio.sharedVideoIds`), into the jam's queue when joined. With the package removed the link goes to the agent composer.

**Dragging onto a deck** starts on the first touch: moving across the queue (sideways in landscape, where the queue is a column; up or down in portrait, where it is a strip) lifts the thumbnail with a haptic tick, while moving along it scrolls. On iOS 18 the drag is a UIKit pan (`DjQueuePan`, a `UIGestureRecognizerRepresentable`) that begins only when heading across and that the scroll view waits on; iOS 17 uses a SwiftUI drag that takes over only when its first movement heads across. A copy follows the finger ("Drop on a deck" / "Load on Deck A/B"), both platters show a dashed ring and the one within 28 points lights up; dropping there loads the video and takes it off the queue, anywhere else puts it back. Thumbnails are kept in memory (`DjQueueThumbs`, not `AsyncImage`) so the copy shows the picture from its first frame. The drag is a gesture in the studio's coordinate space (`DjStudioView.space`), not a system drag session, so it starts quickly inside the full-screen cover; platters report their frames with `onGeometryChange` (`deckFrames`).

The people button above the queue opens the jam sheet. **Start a Jam Session** creates a session with the decks as they are; the host invites ForkMesh users by name (suggestions from `/api/chat/direct-messages/users`), who get a ping and an invitation (a red dot on the people button) the next time they open the studio. Once someone joins:

- the queue is the jam's, and anyone can add or remove; thumbnails show who added each one and when (the time for today, month, day and time before), in the phone's time zone, in full in the menu ("Added by bob on Oct 23, 2026 at 9:41 PM") and to VoiceOver, from each item's `addedAt` (ms; older items have none);
- both decks are shared: every load, START, PAUSE, STOP, RESTART, seek, cue jump, tempo change, SYNC and scratch release is sent as the deck's state (video, playing, position, tempo), debounced 250 ms (`share`). Followers load the same video, match tempo, seek when more than 1.5 s off (0.5 s paused) using the server clock plus half the request's round trip, and start or pause. A deck someone else is changing is not echoed back, and a local change still being sent wins over an older remote one;
- volume, deck levels, crossfader, bend, touch brake and samples stay on each phone, and every phone plays its own copy of the songs (nothing streams between phones);
- the poll is `POST /jams/<id>/presence`, every 1.5 s while the studio is open and 2.5 s while it plays in the background;
- **people icons** (`DjPeopleStrip`, `VideoMixDjStudioParty.swift`): a round icon per person with their initial in a colour from their name, in the queue column (landscape) or a row under it (portrait) and in the jam sheet. The ring is the status (`DjPersonStatus`): green in the studio, blue with headphones listening in the background, grey not listening, dimmed with a moon after 12 s of silence, dashed while invited; the host has a crown;
- **motion**: in a jam with the studio open or music playing, `DjMotionFeed` reads user acceleration (gravity removed) 30 times a second in screen orientation; each presence post carries the strongest of every few samples (12 pairs, ±2 g) and an RMS energy; other phones replay the trace on that person's icon (energy 0.3 or more adds a dancing badge and "dancing" to its label). **Share My Phone's Motion** turns it off (`fm.videoMixDjStudio.shareMotion`, on by default); no location is sent. Reduce Motion keeps icons still;
- **reactions**: the smiley button opens 30 reactions (`DjReaction.all`: hearts, fire, applause, confetti, fireworks, 100, LOL, star-struck, mind blown, lift off, sparkles, music, headphones, dance, groove, hands up, thumbs up, cool, lightning, disco, rainbow, balloons, snow, make it rain, feels, eyes, dead, drop, rock on, love it), each in one of ten styles (rise, rain, burst, zoom, spin, orbit, wave, confetti, fireworks, flash) for 1.6 to 3 s over the studio, with the sender's name and their emoji above their icon. They are silent and flash styles pulse under three times a second; with Reduce Motion each is a single fade. Sent with `POST /jams/<id>/reactions`; others see reactions younger than 8 s on their next poll, each once; outside a jam they play locally. Drawn by one `Canvas` in a `TimelineView` that runs only while a reaction is on screen;
- the host can remove people or end the jam; anyone else can leave. The current jam is remembered (`fm.videoMixDjStudio.jam`) and rejoined when the studio opens.

The server side is the package's server module (`content/worker/video_mix_dj_studio.py`, run by the App; `/api/video-mix-dj-studio`, `dataLifecycle.scope: account`). Tables: `vmdj_jams`, `vmdj_jam_members` (owner / joined / invited), `vmdj_jam_queue`, `vmdj_jam_decks` (one row per side, so two people changing different decks never overwrite each other), `vmdj_jam_presence` (last seen, state `studio` / `listening` / `idle`, playing, energy, motion; rows older than 30 s are not returned) and `vmdj_jam_reactions` (kind and sender, kept 12 s, at most eight per person in that time, else 429 `too_many_reactions`). Titles, queue items, deck states and presence are sealed with `runtime.seal`; a reaction kind is a short id (`[a-z0-9-]{1,24}`). A jam's reply carries `presence` and `reactions` (with `ago` in ms, so phones need no clock agreement) only for joined members. Routes:

| Route | Who |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /jams`, `POST /jams` | list my jams and invitations; create (up to 20 owned) |
| `GET /jams/<id>`, `DELETE /jams/<id>` | read; the host ends it, anyone else leaves |
| `POST /jams/<id>/join`, `POST /jams/<id>/leave` | an invitee joins; a member leaves |
| `POST /jams/<id>/members`, `DELETE /jams/<id>/members/<name>` | members invite (up to 32 people); the host removes |
| `POST /jams/<id>/queue`, `DELETE /jams/<id>/queue/<item>` | members add (up to 100) and remove |
| `PUT /jams/<id>/decks/<a or b>` | members set a deck's state |
| `POST /jams/<id>/presence` | members report state and motion; returns the jam (the poll) |
| `POST /jams/<id>/reactions` | members send a reaction |

Invitees see the jam's people but not its queue or decks until they join. Leaving or removal deletes your presence row. Deleting the account (or removing the package) ends its hosted jams and drops its memberships, presence and reactions (`purge_account_data`).

### Responsiveness

- The platter's gesture bookkeeping lives in a plain object (`DjTouchTrack`), not `@State`, so a drag does not re-evaluate the platter per touch sample.
- The platter's spoken value is Playing / Paused / Empty, and the transport reads the position only while stopped, so player ticks do not redraw a playing deck's platter and transport.
- Artwork is cut to a circle once (`DjArtwork`), not clipped from a 640-pixel image every frame.
- Button haptics use one prepared `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator` (`DjTheme.tick`).
- A jam poll replaces the shared jam only when it changed; presence goes to its own `crowd` list, and a drag redraws the queue only at start and end (`draggedId`).
- The notice bubble animates inside `DjNotice`, not the studio root.
- **Records are Core Animation layers** (`DjSpinner`, `DjSpinView`, `VideoMixDjStudioSpin.swift`), so a busy main thread cannot skip frames of the spin. Artwork, rim dots and label are one rotated layer tree (dots and label drawn into one bitmap per platter size). At steady speed (motor within 0.0005 of the rate, `DjSpinView.settled`) the spin is a repeating `CABasicAnimation` run by the render server at 60 Hz (`DjPace.steady`; 120 Hz held ProMotion at full rate and heated the phone). A `CADisplayLink` checks at 15 Hz that it still matches `DjDeck.turned` and restarts it if it drifts over 0.01 rad (`DjSpinView.slip`) or was removed. Spin-up/down, braking, bend, pitch changes and scratches are driven per frame at up to 120 Hz; a change of rate, touch or scratch wakes the view at once (`updateUIView`). `DjRecordMotion` (`none`, `turn`, `spin`) leaves an unmoved record untouched, and `DjSpinView` writes into the run loop's implicit transaction (an explicit `CATransaction` from a display link flushes the whole window).
- The app sets `CADisableMinimumFrameDurationOnPhone` so ProMotion iPhones can run at 120 Hz; the waveform and reaction timelines are held to 60 Hz (`DjPace.motionInterval`, 0.9 of a frame so a 60 Hz screen gets every frame), the people icons to 24, all halved when the phone is `serious`/`critical` hot or in Low Power Mode; only a touched record asks for 120 Hz (`DjPace`, `FMPackages/DjPace.swift`).
- The waveform keeps moving between record steps: `DjSpin.step` goes back along the spin's speed for a time before its last step (the record view steps up to 67 ms ahead).
- A crossfade saves once at its end and sends both decks' volumes in one script; each jam person icon has its own timeline.

The studio measures itself while on screen (`DjVitals`, `VideoMixDjStudioDiagnostics.swift`): frame times from a 60 Hz probe, main-thread time per piece of studio work, player tick gaps and drift, web view script round trips and what the user did around each long frame. The stethoscope at the end of the bottom row (red with a warning triangle after a freeze, steady stutter or a silent player in the last 30 s) opens Studio diagnostics with Send to an agent; see [video-mix-dj-studio-performance.md](video-mix-dj-studio-performance.md).

### Why both decks can play at once

On iOS, WebKit lets only one `<video>` with sound play at a time across the app: starting one pauses every other (`MediaSessionManageriOS::resetRestrictions` gives the `VideoAudio` type `ConcurrentPlaybackNotPermitted`), and that type is also paused in the background. A muted `<video>` is the `Video` type and an `<audio>` element always `Audio` (`HTMLMediaElement::hasVideo` is true only for `<video>`); neither has the rule. (Moving each deck's video track onto a hidden decoy `MediaSource` only works when YouTube uses MSE, and the never-played decoy kept its buffer, making YouTube rebuffer and loop.) So the frame script makes the restriction impossible to hit:

- every YouTube `<video>` is kept muted for real; `muted` is wrapped to report what YouTube last set. The mute is applied before any `play()` and again on `loadstart`;
- `MediaSource` and `ManagedMediaSource` are hidden inside the YouTube frames, so YouTube uses its plain-stream path, and each deck's sound comes from an `<audio>` "voice" playing the same stream URL, created when YouTube sets the video's source and replaced when it changes;
- the voice plays while the host wants the deck playing (`on`, set by START / PAUSE / STOP, cleared at the song's end), follows every seek YouTube makes and the deck's tempo, and never calls `play()` on an ended element (that would restart the song). A host-requested seek (`w.seek`) moves an ended voice first, so Repeat's restart is heard. The host bumps `gen` on each load; the frame drops the old voice and ignores the old URL until the new one loads;
- the voice is the clock: it reports time, length and state (`voice` messages, four a second while playing), which the host sends to the app instead of YouTube's numbers. The muted video is nudged to the voice at most every three seconds when more than a second apart;
- the voice loads with `crossorigin="anonymous"` so the crossfader can route it; if the stream refuses CORS it reloads without it and that deck can only mute below 2 %. HLS streams are never routed;
- if YouTube reports a playback error with MSE hidden (other than its invalid, removed or not-embeddable codes), the host switches to the decoy path (`fmmse=1`) for that and later players; there a deck's element is unmuted only once its metadata shows no video track.

`document.hidden` and `visibilityState` always report visible and `visibilitychange` is swallowed inside the YouTube frames, so the player never pauses itself when the app is minimized. The script was checked in headless Chromium with a stand-in YouTube frame; desktop browsers do not enforce the iOS rule, so the last check is on a phone.

### Playing in the background

`UIBackgroundModes` includes `audio` and the session stays active while a deck plays, so an `<audio>` voice keeps playing when the app is minimized or locked (it follows `on`, not the video). WebKit suspends every `AudioContext` in the background (`WebAudio` is `BackgroundProcessPlaybackRestricted`), so a routed voice would stop. On entering the background `DjPlayerHost` calls `fmBackground(1)` inside a short background task; a routed voice is swapped for an unrouted one at the same position, and a deck on the decoy path is rebuilt without Web Audio (`bg` stops it routing again). While minimized the crossfader can only mute. `fmBackground(0)` lets decks route again.

To avoid a gap while a fresh voice buffers, the frame keeps a **standby voice** (`standby` in `frameScript`), a muted `<audio>` of the same stream beside a routed voice:

- it exists only while the deck is routed, playing, unmuted, not scratched and in the foreground, and either at full gain or while the app is leaving (`willResignActive` sends `fmAway(1)`, `didBecomeActive` `fmAway(0)`; `away` travels in the frame message), so a deck held part-way on the fader downloads twice only once the app starts to leave;
- it starts 0.2 s ahead and closes the gap by playing up to 15 % slower or faster; it seeks again only when over 0.6 s out, at most every 2 s;
- back at full gain and within 30 ms, the standby is unmuted and the routed voice stopped and disconnected (`swap`), so the deck is unrouted and backgrounding has nothing to hand off; the standby also loads with CORS, so the next fade routes again;
- on entering the background `rescue` unmutes the standby (seeking it first if over 80 ms out) and builds a fresh voice only when there is none. If a standby fails to load, the deck stops making them (`fmLone`).

The waveform `source` message is sent only for a deck's first voice. The decoy path still rebuilds its player on entering the background.

### Opening a push notification while the studio is open

The studio is a `fullScreenCover` above the shell, so a route change beneath it is invisible. `routePushOpen` in `HomeShell` therefore closes the studio first (`DjStudioHub.stepAside`: closes any sheet and hides the cover without popping its page), removes the studio's placeholder page from every tab (`AppRouter.remove(.videoMixDjStudio)`) so going back does not land on an empty page, then routes the tap. Any other route change while the studio shows (a notification action, a link, a project switch) does the same through `onChange(of: router.currentRoute)`. The music keeps playing. Only the studio's own close button pops its page (`leaving` in `VideoMixDjStudioScreen`).

### Reset All

The reset button (right of the bottom row) and the info sheet offer **Reset All** after a confirmation: it stops and unloads both decks, reloads the player web view, puts cues, loops, repeat, tempo, bend, BPM, levels, crossfader, sampler level and samples back to defaults, and saves. Recently Played, playlists and tags are kept. A deck's loop is not restored on relaunch.

## Desktop

The desktop has its own studio (`marketplace/packages/video-mix-dj-studio/content/desktop`, the `video-mix-dj-studio` package module), laid out like VirtualDJ: a top bar, every deck's scrolling waveform across the top, the decks on both sides of a mixer, and a song browser underneath. It does everything the iOS studio does and more.

**Layout.** The top bar has the clock, an engine light, the 2 / 4 decks switch, AUTOMIX, AUTO DJ, MIX NOW, the jam people strip (status rings, a crown for the host, a red dot for invitations), REACT and the studio menu (⚙). Each deck (`DjDeckView`) shows artwork, title, channel, BPM with TAP, elapsed and remaining time (red in the last 30 s), gain, a whole-song overview (click to jump), a loop row, an FX row and eight pads; the jog wheel (`DjPlatter`: the artwork turns as the label, a coloured ring shows progress and blinks in the last 30 s) with SAMPLE, NEXT, find-similar (✦) and file (☰) under it and CUE, play / pause, STOP, RESTART and SYNC below; and the pitch strip (pitch %, range ±8 / 16 / 50, fader, reset, BRAKE, SPIN, − / + bend). Right-hand decks are mirrored. Deck 1 is blue, 2 red, 3 green, 4 orange. In four-deck mode decks 1 and 3 sit left, 2 and 4 right, and the mixer shows channels 3, 1, master, 2, 4.

**Mixer** (`DjMixerView`), four tabs:

- MIXER: per deck GAIN (±12 dB), HIGH / MID / LOW EQ (right-click kills a band; the knob turns red), FILTER (left low-pass, right high-pass), VU meter and volume fader; MASTER, SAMPLER level, AUTOMIX, AUTO DJ and MIX NOW with a stereo master meter. Every knob and fader resets on double-click and takes the wheel (Shift for fine steps).
- VIDEO: the decks' YouTube videos, MIX (the louder deck fades over the other with the crossfader), SPLIT (side by side, 2 × 2 with four decks) or one deck. A transparent cover keeps clicks off YouTube's player.
- SAMPLER: eight drum pads (keys E R T Y U I O P), eight sound effects (air horn, siren, explosion, applause, laugh, rewind, laser, scratch), all synthesized with Web Audio, and the four sample slots.
- AUTOMIX: on / off, AUTO DJ, crossfade length (2 to 30 s, default 8; the mix starts that long plus two seconds before the end), what it plays with the queue empty (also on right-click of any AUTOMIX button), Shuffle and AUTO DJ's next pick.

The crossfader has slam arrows and three curves: smooth (equal power), full (both loud in the middle) and cut (for scratching). Each deck can be assigned `LEFT` / `RIGHT` / `THRU`.

**More than iOS.** Four decks; eight hot cues per deck (set on first press, jump and play after; right-click clears; kept per song in the waveform cache); pad modes HOT CUE, ROLL (hold a 1/8 to 4 beat loop; on release the song carries on where it would have been), SAMPLER and JUMP (±1 to 8 beats); beat loops of 1/8 to 32 beats with IN / OUT (16 beats, about 8 s, without a BPM); key lock; three-band EQ with kills; filter; echo (half a beat at the deck's tempo), reverb and flanger with amount knobs; BRAKE (slows to a stop, pitch dropping) and SPIN (a backspin playing backwards to a stop); master volume; crossfader curves and assignment; the video tab; sound effects; keyboard shortcuts (⚙ → Keyboard Shortcuts); decks 3 and 4, which stay out of jams; and the window's top search box finds and loads library songs.

**Browser** (`DjBrowserView`): a tree of YouTube Search, Queue, Recently Played, Most Played, All Songs, Like the Playing Song (`DjSmart.similar` plus YouTube's suggestions), Playlists, Tags, Smart Playlists and Sampler; a filter box that searches YouTube on Enter or loads a pasted link; a song table with artwork, plays, length and tags; an info panel; and a suggestion line (AUTO DJ's pick and similar songs; click to queue). Double-click or Enter loads onto the deck that would mix in next (the side the crossfader is away from, if not playing), else the first idle deck, else the queue. Songs and browser YouTube links can be dragged onto any deck. Rows have the iOS song menu (plus open or copy the link); lists have Play with AUTOMIX, Add All / Shuffle into the Queue, rename, delete, Save as Playlist and export. Import reads a ForkMesh playlist file, M3U or text with links (right-click IMPORT pastes); export writes the iOS JSON or M3U.

**Behaviour shared with iOS.** The logic is a C++ port of `ForkMeshCore/Sources/FMPackages` (`DjCore` for `DjStudio` and `DjParty`, `DjCrate`, `DjSmart`, `DjAutoDj`, `DjBpm`, `DjWave`, `DjJam`), and `DjModel` follows `DjStudioModel`: transport and playing intent, play counts and Recently Played, `DjEnd`, `DjRepeat`, touch brake and scratching, SYNC and tap tempo, the three-step SAMPLE, AUTOMIX (`DjAutomix.step` on decks 1 and 2), adding to an idle queue starts the song, NEXT, skipping a song moved out of the focus, AUTO DJ, playlists, tags, smart playlists, Don't Shuffle, and jams with the same server module (decks 1 and 2 are A and B; presence every 1.5 s on screen, 2.5 s otherwise; no motion sent; the 30 reactions animate). The pitch range stays ±16 % on decks 1 and 2 in a jam, because the server clamps shared tempos to it. Invidious and thumbnail requests go through the window's network manager (the firewall decides); jam calls go through the host's signed package requests (`/api/video-mix-dj-studio`, proof `forkmesh-video-mix-dj-studio-request-v1`). Invitation suggestions come from the window's user directory.

**Playback engine** (`DjPlayer`, `DjScripts.cpp`). The decks are YouTube IFrame players (`youtube-nocookie.com`) in one hidden `QWebEngineView` with a memory-only profile, loaded with `setHtml` on the server's origin (or `https://forkmesh.com/`). Playback needs no gesture, new windows and navigation are refused, and the `QtWebEngine/x.y` token is removed from the user agent. The page reports through `console.log("fmdj:…")` (`javaScriptConsoleMessage`); the app calls `fmLoad`, `fmCall`, `fmSet`, `fmLayout`, `fmPad` and friends with `runJavaScript`, batched once per event-loop turn. A user script in the YouTube frames (`setRunsOnSubFrames`, main world) does the audio work; desktop YouTube streams through MSE (`blob:` sources), so Web Audio may read it:

- it routes the frame's `<video>` through trim, low shelf (220 Hz), peaking mid (1 kHz), high shelf (3.5 kHz), low-pass and high-pass filters, echo (delay with feedback), reverb (a generated 2.6 s impulse), flanger (an LFO on a short delay) and the fader gain (deck level × crossfader × master), with an analyser for the meters; if routing is impossible the deck falls back to the element's volume;
- tempo is `playbackRate` with `preservesPitch` off unless key lock is on;
- a capture node keeps the last 30 s of audio in a ring buffer indexed by song time and sends it downsampled to 11,025 Hz (mono, 16-bit, base64, every half second) to the app;
- scratching pauses the video and plays the ring buffer from a read head following the hand with a critically damped spring (22 Hz); holding still is silent; release seeks YouTube to the hand; beyond what has been heard is silence. SPIN plays the buffer backwards at 3.6× slowing to a stop; BRAKE ramps `playbackRate` down over 0.9 s; both report where the record stopped;
- `document.hidden` reports visible inside the frames.

The host page ticks every 100 ms (time, length, state and level of every deck), sends titles, and plays drums and effects on its own Web Audio context. When the render process dies the page reloads and every deck is put back.

**Waveforms and BPM.** Desktop YouTube streams through MSE, so there is no plain file to download: `DjModel` builds the waveform from the audio as it is heard, stores the 11,025 Hz audio by song position (up to 15 minutes), measures 20 bars a second (loudness and the bass / mids / treble shares at 250 Hz and 2.5 kHz), and caches bars, BPM, `bpmTapped` and hot cues per song in `data/video-mix-dj-studio/waveforms/<id>.json`, so a song played before shows its whole waveform at once. Detection (`DjBpm.detect`, background thread, hop scaled with the rate: 128 samples at 11,025 Hz) uses only audio captured this session (`DjDeck::captured`), never cached bars with no samples behind them; `bpmQueued` is cleared when detection ends, and a search that found nothing retries after every further 15 s of audio, up to the detector's two-minute window. The scrolling waveforms are a 12 s window (wheel zooms 3 to 60 s, drag scrubs), coloured by band, with a beat grid from the cue when the BPM is known, cue and hot cue markers and the loop region.

**TAP and AUTO** sit under the BPM figure. TAP, the BPM figure and the V and / keys tap the tempo on press: three taps set the BPM (average of the last eight, reset after a 2 s pause); TAP stays lit while counting and the notice says how many more are needed (or asks for a song). AUTO lights when the BPM was detected, blinks while searching or waiting, and a click searches again even over a tapped BPM. With under 15 s heard it waits (the detector needs about 13.7 s at 11,025 Hz: twice eight beats at 70 BPM).

**Saving.** The studio (decks with position, intent, cue, hot cues, tempo, range, BPM, EQ, filter, FX amounts, assignment, key lock and repeat; crossfader, curve, master, sampler level, samples, four-deck mode, video mode, AUTOMIX length, history, played library, queue, AUTOMIX, AUTO DJ and its played list, the jam) goes to `data/video-mix-dj-studio/studio.json` 0.4 s after a change and every 5 s while playing; playlists and tags to `crate.json` (the iOS crate format). The first time the studio is shown after start, decks that were playing start again with AUTOMIX, AUTO DJ and the jam. Leaving the page does not stop the music; while anything is loaded the rail shows a Now playing tile with Stop the music and Mix into the next song. Reset All works as on iOS.

**Without Qt WebEngine** the studio still opens (library, playlists, jams) with a banner saying YouTube cannot play here and a button to the web studio. On Debian and Ubuntu install `qt6-webengine-dev` and rebuild. Package modules link WebEngine when the build has it (`forkmesh_link_webengine` in `desktop/cmake/ForkMeshPackageModules.cmake`); the app already links it.

**Tests.** `forkmesh-video-mix-dj-studio-tests` runs the ports of the iOS core tests (crate, smart playlists, AUTO DJ, BPM at 22,050 and 11,025 Hz, waveform bars, jam parsing) and model checks driven by synthetic player events (play counts, ticks, hot cues, beat loops, curves, the waveform from captured audio, save and restore, the queue starting a song, AUTOMIX, brake, end reports). The engine was also checked against real YouTube in an offscreen Qt WebEngine 6.8 with a fake audio output (two decks routed together; tempo, EQ, echo, scratch, brake and backspin; 113.6 BPM found for a 113 BPM song after 40 s).

## Web

The studio accepts a YouTube URL or video id per deck, detects the watch, share, Shorts, live, embed, attribution and playlist forms, strips extra parameters, embeds the canonical video through YouTube's privacy-enhanced host, and uses the player message protocol for transport, position, tempo and volume. In-studio search uses a healthy public CORS-enabled Invidious index; a result loads its canonical video into the chosen deck. Each overlay announces its purpose and holds keyboard focus until closed, returning focus to its opener; search destinations and result states are exposed to assistive technology. The equal-power crossfader sets each player's volume and matching opacity. Each deck's touch platter pauses, seeks as rotated and resumes on release. Capture stores an eight-second region as a local sampler section that seeks and plays it from its source deck. The Web Audio sampler has eight synthesized percussion pads (`E`, `R`, `T`, `Y`, `U`, `I`, `O`, `P`), so no audio is downloaded, uploaded or kept.

Cues, captured regions, deck level, tempo, sampler level, crossfader, each deck's position and whether it was playing are kept only in `localStorage`; the position is saved every five seconds while playing, on play or pause and when the page is left, and reopening loads each deck there and starts the ones that were playing (the browser may hold sound until the page is touched), as does a back-forward cache restore.

**Auto DJ**, a top-bar toggle kept in `localStorage`, does what AUTO DJ does on iOS with the page's own players: each playing song's recommendations are read from Invidious and filtered the same way (`isSong` / `isOriginal` in `page.js` mirror `DjAutoDj`), the six most related are ranked by likes, and with ten seconds left (or at the end) the best pick loads on the other deck, starts, and the crossfader fades over eight seconds once it plays, then the old deck pauses. A pick YouTube refuses to embed gives way to the next; moving the crossfader by hand stops the fade; a failed lookup retries after 30 seconds. The web page does not use the jam server module yet.

Each deck's controls panel has **Repeat** next to Loop 8s: the song starts over when YouTube reports its end, and AUTO DJ neither looks up nor mixes from that deck. Kept with the deck in `localStorage`, it survives loads and is cleared by Reset.

The page is controlled by each video's availability policy: YouTube advertising, embedding rules and playback restrictions stay under YouTube's and the owner's control.
