How it works

  1. 1

    Write a text ad

    A headline, one sentence, a button label and an https link. No images, no scripts, no tracking parameters. Anyone with an account can write and submit one.

  2. 2

    A person reviews it

    Nothing is approved automatically. A reviewer reads every ad and its destination against the policy, and every decision is recorded. Changing the words sends an ad back to review.

  3. 3

    It runs by surface, not by person

    Ads are chosen by where they appear - never by who is looking. No profiles, no cookies, no impressions counted; only a daily click total per ad and surface.

There is no ad billing yet

A membership level funds a number of live slots and a rotation weight. Anyone can write and submit an ad; an approved ad runs while its owner has an active membership, and pauses by itself when they do not.
