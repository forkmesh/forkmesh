How the estimate works

  1. 1

    Reach score, 0 to 100

    Your ForkMesh follower count, visits already observed from that website, and a bonus when the domain is verified on your profile. ForkMesh never fetches the URL.

  2. 2

    Potential visits

    A range from the score and the channel you pick. It is an estimate, not a forecast.

  3. 3

    SOL appreciation

    0.00005 to 0.0005 SOL by score, paid directly to the submitter's Solana address if someone chooses to. Add yours in Settings.
