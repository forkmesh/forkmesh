How an estimate becomes a payment

  1. 1

    Submit normally

    Open a useful issue or a focused pull request in the repository.

  2. 2

    The owner reviews it

    Duplicates and spam are closed. Useful new reports and focused PRs may receive an estimate.

  3. 3

    It appears as pending

    PR rewards remain conditional until merge; missing payout wallets stay clearly blocked.

  4. 4

    The owner pays locally

    The ForkMesh desktop app unlocks the owner's linked wallet and broadcasts the exact direct transfer.
