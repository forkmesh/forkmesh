Create a new repository

A repository holds your project's files and history.

Where should it live?

Your device keeps the repository. ForkMesh shows it here and lets others find it, but your copy stays the original.

On your computer The ForkMesh desktop app serves the repository from your computer while the app is running.
  1. Open the app's Repos page.
  2. Choose New repository…, or Import folder… for a project you already have.
  3. Turn on Serve for it on the Control page.
Get the desktop app
On your phone The iPhone and Android app keeps the repository on your phone. Publishing sends a copy to the network, so others can clone it while your phone is off.
  1. Open the Repos tab.
  2. Under On this device, tap New repository, or Import from Files.
  3. Open it and tap Publish.
Get the phone app

Already installed? Link the app to this account in Settings › Nodes, and the repository appears in your list once it is published.
