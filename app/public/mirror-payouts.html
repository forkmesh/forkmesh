How it will work

Mirroring keeps the network decentralized. Rewards are an optional thank-you on top of it, never a wage.

Fresh signed presence, a healthy HTTPS endpoint, a verified forkmesh/forkmesh refs proof, no abuse flag, minimum uptime, and a valid public payout address.

03

You may receive rewards

Eligible, healthy nodes may receive configurable community incentives after the local signer and on-chain finality checks. No reward is promised.

Your payout wallet

Publish a public address now so your node is ready when rewards go live.

Non-custodial payout wallet Your payout address belongs to you. Wallet keys and recovery phrases stay on your device; ForkMesh never asks you to upload them. User-owned funds, community-pool funds, pending rewards, and completed on-chain transfers are separate states. The public community pool is signed only by the first instance owner’s local Qt client; the Worker stores unsigned plans and finalized public signatures, never the pool private key. Rewards are community incentives, not investments or guaranteed returns.
