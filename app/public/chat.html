Chat encryption

Private channels and direct messages are encrypted automatically to known participant devices, beyond the controller's room key. Public channels are intentionally controller-readable so ForkBot, MCP, and incoming webhooks can participate.

Every message is signed by the device that wrote it and sealed with post-quantum keys that each device replaces daily and deletes after 8 days. A new device can read only messages sealed after it joined. In a direct message, a new device for someone already in the conversation gets nothing until you approve it. Changing a channel to Public does not expose its previously encrypted messages.
