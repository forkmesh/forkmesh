How this meeting stays private

  • Encrypted on your device. Your audio and video are encrypted in this browser before they leave it, with a key only people holding this meeting's link have.
  • The call server cannot see or hear you. ForkMesh's call server only forwards the encrypted media. Names and the keys each person uses are sealed the same way before they are relayed.
  • The key stays in the link. It is the part after #, which browsers never send to a server. ForkMesh made the link when the meeting was booked and gave it only to the guest and the host. Treat it like a key: anyone you give it to can join.
  • Effects and transcripts stay here. Faces, backgrounds, voices and the transcript all run on this device. ForkMesh records nothing.
