Discourse on ForkMesh

Start a Discourse mirror

Keep a read-only copy of a public Discourse forum in one of your repositories. Its Forum tab fills in over the next hours, stays current every half hour, and every mirror and clone of the repository carries it, so the forum stays readable where the original is blocked.

A forum mirror runs on your own ForkMesh instance. Sign in with its admin account to set one up.
