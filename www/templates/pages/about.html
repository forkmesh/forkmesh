Our story

Your Code, Your World.

ForkMesh lets developers collaborate on, mirror, and preserve source-code repositories across independent hosts—without relying on any single hosting provider.

ForkMesh began with a loss - and a refusal to let it happen again.

Read the story Meet the founders

The day the code disappeared

One ordinary morning, Md Kaif Ansari opened GitHub to find his repositories gone. No warning he could act on, no second copy to fall back on. Years of work - side projects, experiments, the small tools he'd quietly built for himself and others - gone in an instant.

The code wasn't lost because it was wrong. It was lost because it lived in exactly one place.

"If a single account can erase years of work overnight, then we never really owned our code - we were only borrowing it."

So he built the antidote

ForkMesh is what Kaif wished he'd had that morning: a network where your code does not have to depend on a single host. Repositories can be copied to independently operated mirrors, with signed state and integrity checks. External stubs and repositories without a healthy mirror remain clearly labeled; availability improves only when enough authorized, current copies actually exist.

Around that foundation grew everything a modern project needs: issues and milestones, pull requests and releases, encrypted chat, shell-step CI on your own desktop, and AI agents that can pick up an issue and ship a fix - all without surrendering your work to a single point of failure. And because the people who keep the network alive deserve recognition, ForkMesh includes a transparent, non-custodial community reward program. Rewards are voluntary incentives, never guaranteed returns.

Meet the founders

Md Kaif Ansari
Founder & Full-Stack Developer

Md Kaif Ansari is a full-stack developer from India with a passion for building resilient, well-crafted software - and for taking things apart to understand how to make them better. He works primarily across the MERN stack and currently builds full-stack products for an Australian startup.

Beyond his day-to-day work, Kaif is an active open-source contributor drawn to projects with real-world impact, and a dedicated Python and AI enthusiast who treats every hard problem as a puzzle worth solving. A regular on the hackathon circuit, he thrives in fast-paced environments where ambitious ideas become working products - and he approaches every day as a chance to learn and push his craft further.

Jett Vale
Co-Founder & Systems Engineer

Jett Vale is a developer and Kaif's partner in building ForkMesh, with a deep love for the layer most people never see - distributed systems, peer-to-peer networking, and the quiet infrastructure that keeps data alive. He cut his teeth writing performance-critical backends in Rust and Go, and is happiest tracing a packet through a mesh until it does exactly what it should.

When a deleted repository turned into a mission, Jett was the one mapping out how code could live everywhere at once instead of nowhere. He's a tinkerer and an open-source believer who cares as much about clean protocols as he does about the people relying on them - and he's convinced the best software is the kind that simply refuses to disappear.

Where we're headed

Source code is infrastructure. It shouldn't depend on the goodwill of a single host. ForkMesh exists so that the next developer who opens their account to find it empty has somewhere else their work already lives.
