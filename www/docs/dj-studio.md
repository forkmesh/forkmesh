# DJ Studio jams

Video Mix DJ Studio mixes two YouTube decks with turntables, a crossfader,
cues, loops, tempo and sampler pads. Install it once from the Market and it
follows your account to your phone, desktop and the web dashboard. On iPhone
and Android it is a full-screen native studio, and there you can start a
**jam**: friends join, share both decks and the queue, and each phone plays
the music itself.

## The queue

The row of small video thumbnails in the middle of the studio is the queue.

- **Add a song.** Press **+**: the loader opens with **Queue** chosen, and the
  search result you pick goes to the queue instead of a deck. On iPhone you
  can also share a YouTube link to ForkMesh from the share sheet.
- **Plays at once when idle.** If nothing is playing, a song you add does not
  wait: it loads and starts straight away, on the deck the crossfader favours
  (or on the empty deck, so a song you cued is not replaced). Adding a
  playlist or tag plays its first song this way and queues the rest.
- **Hold for a menu.** Tap or hold a thumbnail for Load on Deck A, Load on
  Deck B, Tags and Playlists… and Remove from Queue. Loading a song takes it
  off the queue.
- **Drag onto a deck.** Move your finger across the queue (sideways in
  landscape, up or down in portrait) and the thumbnail lifts at once. Drop it
  on a platter to load it there; drop it anywhere else to put it back. Moving
  along the queue still scrolls it.
- **AUTOMIX** takes the next song from the queue when it mixes.

Outside a jam the queue is kept on your phone.

## Start a jam

1. Press the people button at the top of the queue and choose **Start a Jam
   Session**. The decks carry on as they are.
2. Invite ForkMesh users by name; names are suggested as you type.
3. They get a ping, and a red dot on the people button the next time they
   open the studio. Once they join, the jam is shared.

## In a jam

- **One queue for everyone.** Anyone in the jam can add or remove songs. Each
  thumbnail shows who added it and when: just the time for today, the date
  and time before that, in your phone's time zone. The menu says it in full,
  for example "Added by bob on Oct 23, 2026 at 9:41 PM". Songs queued before
  this was added show no time.
- **Shared decks.** Every load, start, pause, stop, seek, tempo change and
  scratch is shared, and the other phones follow within a second or two.
  Nothing is streamed between phones: each one plays its own copy from
  YouTube. Your deck volume stays your own.
- **People.** Everyone has a round icon. Green means in the studio, blue with
  headphones listening in the background, grey not listening, a moon not
  heard from for a while, dashed still invited. The host has a crown.
- **Motion and reactions.** Icons move when people dance with their phone.
  Turn this off with **Share My Phone's Motion** in the jam sheet; no location
  is ever sent. The smiley button sends one of 30 silent reactions that play
  over everyone's studio.
- **Leaving.** The host can remove people or end the jam for everyone; anyone
  else can leave. Your phone remembers the jam and rejoins it when you open
  the studio again.

Limits: up to 32 people in a jam, 100 songs in its queue, and 20 jams you
host. Playlists stay on your phone; only the songs you queue from them are
shared. Removing the package, or deleting your account, ends the jams you
host.

## On Android

Android has the studio and jams. The newest queue changes (hold for a menu,
drag on first touch, play at once when idle, and who added each song and
when) arrive on iPhone first and are still being ported. See
[ForkMesh apps](apps.md).
