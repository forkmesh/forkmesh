# ForkMesh World Song

The World entrance sign links to *ForkMesh Forever (Indie Pop)*, a first-party
track published by the project at
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EsQMZuS6lbE>. Clicking the sign opens that
video in a new tab; nothing is downloaded, decoded, or played by the World
itself, and no audio file is bundled with or served from ForkMesh’s own origin.

All other World sounds are short event effects synthesized locally with the
Web Audio API. No ambient music catalog or procedural soundtrack is bundled.
