How listings work

Descriptive, not a ranking

Each listing says what a project does, why we follow it and how it relates to ForkMesh today. The ForkMesh team reviews every listing, and each project page says when we last checked it. Project names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Building something in this space, or spotted a mistake about your project? Email founders@forkmesh.com and we will add or correct the listing.

Compatible
Whether the project works with ForkMesh today: not yet assessed, not compatible, partial or full.
Integration
Where ForkMesh stands on connecting to it: watching, exploring, planned, in progress or available. Services, libraries and tools that ForkMesh itself runs on are marked used by ForkMesh.
Git-native
The project hosts, replicates or stores its collaboration data in Git.
Compared with ForkMesh
Facts about the project and ForkMesh side by side, topic by topic. It shows where they differ or overlap, not which is better.
