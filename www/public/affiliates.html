Rules

The contest rules, kept

These are the September Revenue Contest’s rules, minus the leaderboard and the end date.

Claims

  • You may claim a maximum of 10 companies at a time.
  • Claim a company here before any outreach. The first valid claim gets the company. We go by its website address.
  • Claims expire after 7 days without meaningful outreach or activity. Log what you did to keep yours.

What counts

  • Referrals must be new ForkMesh paid customers and cannot already be in an active ForkMesh sales conversation.
  • One customer counts as one referral. Related accounts or entities may be treated as one customer.
  • A referral counts after successful payment through Patreon. Refunds, chargebacks, fraudulent payments or reversals do not count.
  • The customer must pay with their own payment method. A payment made or funded by you, or by anyone acting for you, does not count.
  • Subscriptions must stay paid and in good standing for 30 days before a commission is final.

Fair play

  • No self-referrals, fake accounts, duplicate customers, artificial upgrades, or coordinated purchases.
  • Only give us someone’s contact details if they are happy for us to have them. No scraped lists and no spam in our name.
  • ForkMesh may verify referrals and resolve disputes, duplicate claims, or suspected abuse.
  • ForkMesh may modify or clarify these rules. Changes will be communicated publicly.

Your claims

    Questions

    Who can join?

    Anyone with a ForkMesh account and a verified email address. You do not need to be a customer yourself.

    Is this the revenue contest?

    It is the same deal without the end date or the leaderboard. The contest ended on 15 October and its page is in the hackathon archive. Commission, claims and fair-play rules carry over unchanged.

    What counts as activity on a claim?

    Real outreach: an email sent, a call, a demo, a follow-up. Log it on the claim in a sentence. Each entry keeps the claim for another 7 days. Logging nothing for 7 days lets it lapse, and then anyone can claim the company.

    Someone else already claimed a company I know. Can I still get credit?

    No. The first valid claim wins, which is the only way to keep this fair. If their claim lapses, you can claim the company yourself.

    Why does it say a company is already known?

    The contact you gave is already in our CRM, so we are already talking to them. That claim does not earn commission, and it does not use up one of your 10.

    What happens to the details I send?

    They go into our CRM with your name as the source, so our team knows the company is yours. We do not sell them or add anyone to a newsletter. If someone asks us to, we delete their details.

    How do I get paid?

    Once a commission is final we email you at your account address to agree how to send it. You will see the amount next to the company here first.
