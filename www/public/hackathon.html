Who can enter

Four checks, for builders and raters alike

A rating only counts when the person leaving it has passed all four checks, the same as the people building. That keeps the race between real people.

  1. 1

    A ForkMesh account

    With a verified email address and in good standing. Make one here if you do not have one yet.

  2. 2

    Our member check

    Your account is at least 7 days old, and the desktop app on your computer has proved its key on two days, a day apart. It is the same check we use before a referral counts.

  3. 3

    Post about it three times

    One public post each on X, Reddit and Mastodon, with #ForkMeshPluginRace in it. Say what you plan to build, or what you want someone else to build.

  4. 4

    A Discord account

    Join the ForkMesh Discord and give us your username. Builders help each other there, and it is where we announce winners.

        Questions

        Why do raters have to qualify too?

        Because ten ratings from throwaway accounts would win the race for whoever made the most accounts. Asking everyone to pass the same checks means ten ratings are ten real people.

        What is the member check, exactly?

        Your account has to be at least a week old, and the ForkMesh desktop app on your computer has to have proved its signing key to us on two occasions at least a day apart, and been online in the last two days. Install the desktop app, sign in, and leave it running for a day.

        You could not check one of my posts. Now what?

        Some networks do not let us read a post from our servers. When that happens the post shows as waiting and someone on the team checks it by hand, usually within a day. You do not need to do anything.

        Do low ratings count?

        Ratings of 3 stars and up count toward the 10. Lower ratings still appear in the marketplace and still help you improve the plugin, they just do not move you up the board.

        Can my friends rate my plugin?

        Yes, if they pass the four checks and have actually used it. Trading ratings, paying for them, or asking for five stars in return for anything gets everyone involved disqualified.

        Do I need a crypto wallet?

        Only if you want the prize. Any Solana address you control works. Save it in your account settings. We never ask for keys or seed phrases, and nobody from ForkMesh will ever ask you for one.

        Where did the revenue contest go?

        It ended on 15 October. Its page and rules are in the hackathon archive, and the commission carries on in the affiliate program.
