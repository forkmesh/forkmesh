ForkMesh is on Open Collective

ForkMesh has a page on Open Collective now, at opencollective.com/forkmesh. It went up today and it's still mostly empty. Here's what it's for and what's on it so far.

September 28, 2026Announcement2 min read

The short version: opencollective.com/forkmesh exists. Go have a look, and follow it if you want to see it fill in.

Why Open Collective

Everything else about ForkMesh is already out in the open. The code is public at forkmesh/forkmesh, the issues are public, and this blog tells you what changed and why. Money is the one part you couldn't easily look at.

Open Collective is built for exactly that. A project's page shows who is behind it, and as money comes in and goes out, each payment and each expense shows up on the page where anyone can read it. We'd like the money side of ForkMesh to be as easy to look at as the code, and this is the first step.

What's on it right now

Not much yet, and we'd rather say so. The screenshot at the top of this post is the whole page. It has a name, a one-line description ("code collaboration, mirroring and preservation across independent nodes"), a link back to forkmesh.com, and two admins from the ForkMesh team.

It's set up as an organization account. There are no contribution tiers yet, the balance is $0, and the one button that does anything is Submit expense. The logo is a placeholder gray letter F for now.

What doesn't change

Nothing about how you support ForkMesh today. Supporter and Pro still go through Patreon, and Solana donations work the same as before. When the Open Collective page can do more, we'll say so here first.

Tell us what you'd want on it

If there's something you'd want to see on the page, like a particular kind of spending broken out or a way to fund specific work, tell us in the ForkMesh Discord or open an issue on forkmesh/forkmesh. Thanks for reading.
