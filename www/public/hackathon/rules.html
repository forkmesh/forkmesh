ForkMesh September Revenue Contest Official rules

Official rules

These rules govern the contest. Where anything on another ForkMesh page disagrees with this document, this document is what applies.

1. Organiser and contact

The ForkMesh September Revenue Contest is run by ForkMesh. Questions about these rules go to founders@forkmesh.com.

2. Dates

The contest runs from 15 September 2026, 00:00 UTC to 15 October 2026, 23:59 UTC. All times are UTC.

3. Who can enter

The contest is open to everyone. You bring new companies onto a paid ForkMesh plan through Patreon, earn commission, and compete for cash prizes.

4. Commission

You earn 50% of each eligible customer’s first month.

  • $5 plan: $2.50
  • $10 plan: $5
  • $50 plan: $25
  • $100 plan: $50

Customers pay through Patreon. The Patreon link is on the ForkMesh homepage.

5. Leaderboard and prizes

Rankings are based on total eligible first-month revenue generated, not number of signups.

  • 1st: $100, with a minimum of $200 in eligible first-month revenue
  • 2nd: $50, with a minimum of $100 in eligible first-month revenue
  • 3rd: $25, with a minimum of $50 in eligible first-month revenue

To win any prize you also need at least 3 different eligible customers. A prize is never more than half of the revenue that earns it.

  • Places are awarded in leaderboard order. If you rank for a place but are below its minimum, you take the highest lower place whose minimum you meet.
  • A place that no entrant qualifies for is not awarded and does not pass to anyone below its minimum.
  • Prize minimums do not affect commissions. Every eligible referral still earns 50% of its first month.

Prizes are paid in addition to commissions.

6. Claiming companies

  • You may claim a maximum of 10 companies at a time.
  • Claims must be posted publicly before outreach to prevent overlap. The first valid public claim gets the company.
  • Claims expire after 7 days without meaningful outreach or activity.

7. What counts as a referral

  • Referrals must be new ForkMesh paid customers and cannot already be in an active ForkMesh sales conversation.
  • One customer counts as one referral. Related accounts or entities may be treated as one customer when appropriate.
  • A referral counts after successful payment through Patreon. Refunds, chargebacks, fraudulent payments, or reversals do not count.
  • The customer must pay with their own payment method. A payment made or funded by the entrant, or by anyone acting for the entrant, does not count.
  • Each customer counts once, at the price of the plan they first pay for.
  • Subscriptions must remain paid and in good standing for 30 days before commissions and contest revenue are final.

8. Fair play

No self-referrals, fake accounts, duplicate customers, artificial upgrades, or coordinated purchases intended to manipulate the contest.

9. Verification and disputes

ForkMesh may verify referrals and resolve disputes, duplicate claims, or suspected abuse.

10. Changes

ForkMesh may modify or clarify rules as needed. Changes will be communicated publicly.

On 1 October 2026 we added the prize minimums in section 5 and the payment rules in section 7, so that a single small payment cannot win a prize. Commissions are unchanged, and no prize had been awarded before this change.

On 6 October 2026, the commission table was corrected to replace a $25 example that did not match a Patreon tier with the $10 tier, and to add the $50 tier it had left out. The 50% commission rate did not change.
