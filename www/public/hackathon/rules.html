ForkMesh Plugin Race Official rules

Official rules

These rules govern the Plugin Race. Where anything on another ForkMesh page disagrees with this document, this document is what applies.

1. Organiser and contact

The ForkMesh Plugin Race is run by ForkMesh. Questions go to hello@forkmesh.com or the ForkMesh Discord.

2. Dates

The race opens on 19 October 2026, 00:00 UTC, and closes on 18 December 2026, 23:59 UTC, or as soon as all ten prizes have been won, whichever comes first.

3. The prize

  • The first 10 plugins to reach 10 counted ratings each win 0.1 SOL.
  • That is ten prizes of 0.1 SOL, and one prize per plugin.
  • Each builder may enter up to 3 plugins, and each one can win.
  • We send the prize to the Solana address saved in your ForkMesh account settings, within 14 days of confirming the win. The transaction signature goes up on the race board.
  • If you have not saved an address within 30 days of winning, the prize lapses and the slot passes to the next plugin in line.

4. Who can take part

Everyone in the race, whether you build a plugin, rate one, or both, has to pass all four checks before anything they do counts:

  1. A ForkMesh account with a verified email address, in good standing.
  2. Our member check. The account is at least 7 days old, and the ForkMesh desktop app on your computer has proved its signing key on two separate days at least a day apart and has been online in the last 48 hours. This is the same check we use before a referral counts. A new email address alone does not pass it.
  3. Three public posts about the race: one on X, one on Reddit and one on Mastodon. Each must be public and include #ForkMeshPluginRace. One post can only be used by one person.
  4. A Discord account. Give us your Discord username and join the ForkMesh Discord. If your Discord is already linked to your ForkMesh account it counts at once. If not, we check it by hand.

You enter on the race page while signed in. The page shows which checks you have passed. We check the posts automatically where the network lets us, and by hand where it does not.

ForkMesh staff and contractors may build and rate but cannot win a prize.

5. What the plugin has to be

  • Published by you in the ForkMesh community marketplace.
  • Useful: it does a real job for someone using ForkMesh. Themes with no function, copies of an existing package, and placeholder packages are not accepted.
  • Working on all three places ForkMesh runs: the desktop app, the iOS app, and the web dashboard. When you enter, you tell us how to try it on each one.
  • Built for the race. A package that was already published before 19 October 2026 can enter only with a new release made during the race.
  • Safe: no tracking, no hidden network calls, nothing that breaks the marketplace rules.

We review every entry and either accept it or tell you why not. Only accepted plugins appear on the board and can win. Ratings left before acceptance still count once the plugin is accepted.

6. Which ratings count

  • The rating is left in the ForkMesh marketplace during the race.
  • The person rating has passed all four checks in section 4.
  • The rating is 3 stars or more.
  • Each person counts once per plugin. You cannot rate your own plugin.
  • Ratings we hide for abuse, and ratings from anyone we disqualify, stop counting.
  • A rating counts from the later of two moments: when it was left, and when the person rating passed the checks. The tenth counted rating sets the time a plugin crossed the line.

The first ten accepted plugins to cross the line win, in that order. If two cross at exactly the same moment, the one whose package name comes first alphabetically goes first.

7. Fair play

  • Swapping ratings, buying or selling ratings, rating rings, second accounts, and offering anything in return for a rating are not allowed. Everyone involved is disqualified.
  • Rate what you actually used. An honest 3-star rating counts as much as a 5.
  • Your posts have to be your own and stay up until the race ends. Deleting one takes you out of the race.
  • We may disqualify an entrant, remove a plugin from the board, or withhold a prize when we find abuse, including after the fact.

8. Checks and decisions

ForkMesh checks every entrant, post, plugin and rating, and settles disputes. Our decisions on eligibility and winners are final. Prizes are sent in SOL; you are responsible for any tax on them where you live. The race is void where the law does not allow it.

9. Changes

ForkMesh may clarify or change these rules. Any change is posted on this page and announced on our Discord, with the date it was made. A change never takes away a prize that has already been won.
