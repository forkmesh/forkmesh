Install Sugar Break

Start a sugar break of a month or any length you like, then press +1 every time you skip sugar. Watch your sugar-free days, streak, and progress add up. Your break is private to your account and syncs across the web dashboard, the desktop app, and the mobile app, and on iPhone it can read the sugar Apple Health records. Every break is encrypted at rest with a key minted for your account alone.
