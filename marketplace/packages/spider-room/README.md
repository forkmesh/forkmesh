# Spider Room

The Genie spider leaves the screen and comes into your room. Open **Spider
Room** on the iPhone, move the phone slowly around, and the spider lowers
itself on a silk thread onto the floor in front of you.

- **Throw it.** Drag the spider to pick it up and flick to throw it. It sticks
  to whatever it hits: a wall, a table top, the ceiling. A hard throw leaves it
  dizzy, and a drop onto the floor bounces.
- **Send it somewhere.** Tap the floor, a wall or a piece of furniture and it
  walks there, climbing up walls, leaping onto tables and seats and lowering
  itself back down on silk.
- **Tickle it.** Tap the spider and it giggles.
- **It plays with the room.** It naps on a sofa or chair, dances on tables,
  knocks on doors, peeks out of windows, hangs from the ceiling and spins webs
  on walls. **Clear webs** takes them down.

On phones with a LiDAR scanner, furniture in front of the spider hides it.
Android has no Spider Room yet.

Free, bundled with the iPhone app, off by default. Camera frames stay on the
phone; nothing is saved or sent. Developer notes:
