Password Vault

Logins encrypted in this browser before they leave it. ForkMesh stores ciphertext and cannot read a title, a username, or a password. Share one with a ForkMesh user or with an organization you belong to.

This browser cannot open a vault

The vault needs X25519 and Ed25519 in the browser's own cryptography. Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari have both. Update this browser, then reload.

Create your vault

Choose a master password. It never leaves this browser and ForkMesh cannot reset it, so it must be one you will not forget and do not use anywhere else. Four or more unrelated words work well.

At least 12 characters.

Unlock your vault

Stretching the master password takes a few seconds on purpose: it is what makes guessing it expensive.

Who this reaches

Your recovery code

Shown once

This is the only way back into the vault if the master password is forgotten. It is shown once. Print it or write it down and keep it somewhere safe, away from this device. Do not store it in the vault.

Recover your vault

The recovery code opens the vault once. You set a new master password and get a new code; the old one stops working.
