Browser Extension

One ForkMesh extension for Chrome. It fills and saves logins from your Password Vault, links the mail you read to your organization's CRM, and fills Marketing's Reddit desk from the Reddit pages you open, from a single sign-in.

Install

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. It holds one folder, browser-extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick that folder.
  3. Click the ForkMesh toolbar icon and sign in with your ForkMesh account. The extension keeps its own session; it never reads this site's cookies.
  4. To update, unzip the newer download over the same folder and press Reload on the extension's card.

Loading it from a ForkMesh source checkout? Pick marketplace/packages/browser-extension/content/extension, not marketplace/packages/browser-extension: that package folder holds the Market manifest, and Chrome rejects it with Missing 'manifest_version' key.

Coming from the older ForkMesh CRM extension? It was loaded from a crm-extension folder. Remove that one and load this one: it does everything the old one did.

Password Vault

Unlock the vault in the popup with your master password. On a site with a saved login, press Fill. The extension types it into that tab only, and only when the tab's host is exactly the login's. It can also save the login you just typed and generate passwords. Keys stay in memory, lock after 15 idle minutes, and are gone when the browser closes.

CRM

In Gmail, Zoho Mail and the Mastodon instances you add, a CRM header sits above the open message and links it to a contact in one click, and mail you send there to someone already in the CRM is linked to their record by itself. The popup also files any page under a contact's notes. Needs the CRM package installed for your organization.

Marketing

On reddit.com it reads the watched subreddits, watched searches, threads and rules pages you open yourself, and the popup's Send to Reddit desk logs them as threads to review on the Marketing Reddit desk. It never posts, votes or sends anything to Reddit. On x.com it reads your organization's profile counts for the X channel. On those sites and your Marketing channels' sites, the toolbar icon counts the pages left to read, and its side panel has a button for each page and one that refreshes the site's data, without adding anything to the site. Needs the Marketing package installed for your organization.
