# Booking Page

Booking Page is an optional ForkMesh package that any account installs for
itself: a public page with one short link, such as
`forkmesh.com/meet?alice`, that you send to anyone who wants to meet you.

- **Meeting types.** Offer a quick chat, a meeting, a phone call or anything
  else, each with its own length, description and place (a video link, a
  phone number, an address). Every type also has its own link, such as
  `forkmesh.com/meet?alice/intro`.
- **Only free times.** You set your weekly hours, time zone, the shortest
  notice, how far ahead people can book, a gap between meetings and a daily
  limit. Times that clash with your ForkMesh calendar or another booking are
  never offered.
- **Booked in a few clicks.** The guest picks a day and a time in their own
  time zone, leaves their name, email and an optional note, and is done. The
  confirmation page has Google, Outlook and Apple calendar buttons.
- **Everything through ForkMesh.** The meeting goes on your ForkMesh calendar
  with reminders. ForkMesh emails the guest a confirmation and a reminder the
  day before, emails you the booking, and pings you in ForkMesh. Replies to
  the guest's email reach you; replies to yours reach the guest.
- **Change or cancel.** The guest's email has a private link to reschedule or
  cancel; you can cancel from the dashboard with a note. Both sides are
  emailed.

Settings, bookings and guest details are encrypted under a key minted for
your account. Uninstall downloads an export first, then deletes your page and
bookings; meetings already on your calendar stay there. Developer notes are in
[docs/marketplace/booking.md](../../../docs/marketplace/booking.md).
