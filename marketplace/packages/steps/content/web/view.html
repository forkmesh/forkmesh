Pedometer

Steps your phone counts, synced through Apple Health to your account on the web, desktop, and mobile.

Install Pedometer

Install it here, then open Steps in the ForkMesh iPhone app and turn on step counting. The app reads your steps from Apple Health and the motion sensor and sends each day's total to your account, so you can watch them from any computer. Every day is encrypted at rest with a key minted for your account alone.

Add steps by hand

For a walk your phone missed. They count on top of Apple Health's total for that day.

Goal

Pedometer data

Export your step history as JSON, or uninstall Pedometer from your account. Uninstalling downloads your export first, then deletes your steps on web, desktop, and mobile. Apple Health keeps its own copy.
