Green dots for the ForkMesh systems you watch and for your own sites. Your checks, monitors and settings follow your account to the desktop and mobile apps.

Install Status Page

Shows ForkMesh's live status as green dots on the web, desktop and mobile apps, with a check for each system and your own monitors. Installing it here installs it on every device signed in to your account, with the default checks switched on.

Status

      Checks

      Each check is its own package and adds one or more dots. Switching one off removes its dots from every device.

      Settings

      Your monitors

      Each of your devices loads these addresses itself and shows a green dot while they answer.

      Remove Status Page

      Removes the dots, every check and your monitors from the web, desktop and mobile apps.
